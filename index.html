The SDP Experience Center
Concept Presentation

The Vision

A 5-minute enclosed walkthrough designed to showcase the scale, precision, and engineering dominance of SDP machinery. Visitors step directly inside the cutting and polishing process through large-scale visual installations that make your equipment feel larger than life.

1. Entrance: The Crowd Puller (The "WOW" Portal)

The Setup

A large 3-sided screen archway (left wall, right wall, and ceiling) framing the entry directly off the expo aisle.

What Visitors Experience

From the aisle, the portal looks like a deep industrial quarry. A massive 30-ton raw granite block hangs suspended by heavy chains.

The block swings violently forward toward the aisle, appearing to break through the screen boundary and hover directly over the crowd before pulling back inside.

The Objective

Stop foot traffic in the aisle immediately and pull qualified buyers inside the booth.

2. Zone 1: Granite Block Cutter (SDP 2300 M-05)

The Setup

Corner display (two vertical walls meeting at 90 degrees) capped by an overhead ceiling screen.

What Visitors Experience

Overhead Drop: Five massive 2.5-meter diamond blades spin up across the ceiling screen and visually drop downward into the corner space above the viewer.

Zero-Vibration X-Ray: The blades strike a block of granite. The machine casing turns transparent, showing a glowing X-ray view of the heavy cast-iron pillar frame absorbing the impact shock with zero vibration.

The Under-Slice: The camera dives directly beneath the cut. In ultra-slow motion, diamond teeth slice quartz cleanly with zero chipping, while water spray erupts across the walls.

The Sales Message

Proves why SDP's heavy cast-iron build delivers a cleaner cut and longer blade life than lighter competitor frames.

3. Zone 2: Automatic Bridge Multi-Cutter (SJ-21)

The Setup

A 3-sided tunnel (left wall, right wall, and overhead ceiling screen).

What Visitors Experience

The Gantry Drive-Over: The massive steel bridge gantry rolls along wall tracks at high speed, passing directly over the visitor’s head to show off its sheer operational scale.

21-Blade Peripheral Cut: As the bridge passes overhead, all 21 diamond blades spin at eye level on both side walls, throwing high-pressure cooling mist across the tunnel.

The Aftermath: The machine clears, and the dark tunnel snaps into a brightly lit perspective of an endless warehouse filled with stacked, razor-flat stone slabs.

The Sales Message

Demonstrates the speed, production volume, and multi-blade cutting capacity of SDP bridge cutters.

4. Zone 3: Line Polishing Machine (Rapid Plus 2200)

The Setup

The Polishing Press (a durable, walkable floor screen paired with an overhead curved ceiling screen, leaving the sides open).

What Visitors Experience

The Squeeze: Visitors stand on an unpolished, rough granite slab displayed on the floor screen. Overhead, 21 heavy mechanical polishing heads spin up and lower toward them.

The Water Blast: High-pressure water and polishing compound visually blast down from the ceiling heads, splashing across the floor around the visitor's feet.

The Mirror Reveal: The polishing heads retract and the water drains away. The floor beneath their feet instantly transforms into a hyper-realistic mirror finish, reflecting the ceiling heads and creating deep vertical perspective.

The Sales Message

Visually demonstrates the transformation from raw stone to a flawless, export-grade mirror polish achieved by the Rapid Plus line.

5. Finale: Engineering Convergence & Brand Reveal

The Setup

Seamless L-shaped corner screen positioned at the final turn leading to the lounge.

What Visitors Experience

Heavy internal components from the machines—diamond segments, heavy cast-iron shafts, PLC consoles, and polishing bricks—float outward in forced-perspective 3D space.

The parts magnetically lock together in mid-air to forge the metallic SDP logo, gleaming under directional lights before smoothly looping.

The Objective

Leaves attendees with a memorable final impression of SDP's build quality before they transition directly into the sales lounge and meeting hub.
