Spot 2: Immersion &
Conversion Space

Architectural Layout (24m x 11m)

Executive Lounge (Right)

A spacious, premium VIP area designed to comfortably seat 40–45 people. Furnished entirely with deep-cushion sofas, armchairs, and low-profile coffee tables to maintain a relaxed, uncrowded environment for high-end sales.

Immersion Zone (Left)

An open-flow, high-tech area featuring a central circular VR seating hub. Three freestanding interactive kiosks are spaced across the floor, leaving the perimeter walls completely empty to draw 360-degree foot traffic.

Freestanding Phygital Kiosks (Interactive Hardware)

The Pressure Slider (Blade Precision)

Visitors push a heavy physical slider to drive a digital blade through a real stone block on screen. Gamifying the cut proves how the machine auto-regulates feed rates and pressure to guarantee a flawless finish without jamming.

The Digital Buffer (Polishing Line)

Visitors physically scrub a screen covering a rough, unpolished piece of stone. The action triggers 3D polishing heads and water sprays that digitally "wipe away" the rough texture, revealing a hyper-glossy mirror finish over the raw stone beneath.

The Precision Challenge (Bridge Cutter)

Visitors use touchscreen laser lines to try and cut a perfect grid of 8 equal tiles by eye. After grading their human error, the system instantly auto-calibrates a flawless 100% yield cut, providing immediate proof of the value of automated laser precision.

VR Engineering Hub (No-Friction Virtual Reality)

The Drone Flight

A cinematic virtual journey following a raw stone block from extraction at the quarry all the way through the factory pipeline to become final polished tiles.

Live Factory Floor

A raw, immersive perspective placing the buyer directly on a noisy, active production floor to watch machines operate at maximum speed.

The Exploded Showroom

A quiet, clean virtual environment where users push a single button to strip away the outer steel casings of the machines, revealing the heavy cast-iron engineering and internal drivetrain at work.
